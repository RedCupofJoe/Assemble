# Deal Phases — Definitions & Default Role Mapping

Use these definitions to classify the deal. Signals are what the *customer*
says or does, not what the seller hopes.

## 1. Prospecting
- **Goal:** Get a first conversation; confirm there may be a fit.
- **Customer signals:** Cold or barely engaged; no stated problem yet; vague
  replies; "send me some info."
- **Default helpers:** SDR/BDR lead, Marketing (for tailored collateral),
  Solutions Engineer (only for technical cold outreach).

## 2. Qualification
- **Goal:** Confirm budget, authority, need, timeline; decide to invest time.
- **Customer signals:** Asks "how does this work for a company like ours?",
  mentions an internal process or budget cycle, brings in a colleague.
- **Default helpers:** Account Executive (deal owner), Sales Engineer (fit
  check), Customer Success (reference customers in same industry).

## 3. Discovery
- **Goal:** Map the customer's problems, stakeholders, and success criteria.
- **Customer signals:** Shares pain points, current-tool frustrations, KPIs;
  asks detailed "can you do X" questions.
- **Default helpers:** Solutions Engineer / Product Specialist (deep
  product answers), Industry/Domain Expert, AE (to quantify impact).

## 4. Demo / Evaluation (POC / Trial)
- **Goal:** Prove the product solves their specific problem.
- **Customer signals:** Asks for a trial, test data, security review, or
  "show us exactly how it handles our workflow."
- **Default helpers:** Solutions Engineer (demo/POC design), Support/DevOps
  (sandbox setup), Product Manager (feature gaps & roadmap).

## 5. Proposal
- **Goal:** Present the commercial and solution package for decision.
- **Customer signals:** Asks for pricing, a written proposal, business case,
  or ROI numbers to take internally.
- **Default helpers:** AE (ownership), Sales Engineer (solution write-up),
  Finance / Deal Desk (pricing structure), Value Engineer (ROI model).

## 6. Negotiation
- **Goal:** Agree on terms, price, legal, and security.
- **Customer signals:** Procurement involved; pushes on discounts, liability,
  contract terms; "legal is reviewing."
- **Default helpers:** Deal Desk / Finance, Legal Counsel, senior leader
  (exec sponsor for concession trade).

## 7. Closing
- **Goal:** Signature. Remove last friction, keep momentum.
- **Customer signals:** "Send the paperwork", countersignature pending, asks
  about start dates and onboarding.
- **Default helpers:** AE, Legal (final docs), Onboarding lead (handoff date).

## 8. Onboarding / Expansion
- **Goal:** Deliver value fast; grow the account.
- **Customer signals:** Asks about training, rollout plans, other teams or
  use cases; reports early wins or issues.
- **Default helpers:** Customer Success Manager, Support, Product (feedback
  loop), AE (expansion mapping).

## Classification tips
- Match on the customer's **latest** signals, not the history of the deal.
- Deals can regress (e.g., a new stakeholder restarts Discovery) — classify on
  evidence, not on how long the deal has been open.
- If signals span two phases, choose the later phase only when its defining
  signal is explicit (e.g., procurement engaged = Negotiation).
